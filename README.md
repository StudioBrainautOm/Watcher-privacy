Watcher ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your personal information and data in our mobile application, Watcher (the "App").

1. Information We Collect
Watcher does not collect any personally identifiable information from users. We do not require registration, login, or any form of user identification to use the App.

However, we may use the following types of data:

Local storage data: Your activity logs (e.g., time tracked per keyword) are stored locally on your device.

Preferences: Settings such as language, theme, and notifications are saved locally to improve your experience.

2. How We Use Your Information
Since we do not collect data externally, your information never leaves your device. The App only uses the data to:

Provide time tracking features

Save your preferences (e.g., dark mode, sound alert)

Display statistics and logs

3. Third-Party Services
Watcher does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

If future updates include any integrations (e.g., cloud sync, ads), this policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Children's Privacy
Watcher does not target children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review it periodically.
